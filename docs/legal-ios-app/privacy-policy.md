# Spell-Bee iOS Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026

RuntimeSquad ("we", "us") provides Spell-Bee for iOS. Contact us at [runtimesquad@gmail.com](mailto:runtimesquad@gmail.com) for privacy questions, support, or requests concerning your information.

## Scope

This policy covers the native Spell-Bee iOS app as currently implemented. The separate Spell-Bee website has its own privacy notice. The iOS app's released-build configuration supports offline Adventure and private daily play; production online play is not currently connected. Development simulator features are described separately below.

## Information on your device

The app stores spelling answers and round progress, scores, stars, honey, boosters, accessories, daily results, sound preferences, and your selected family or 13+ experience on your device. We use this information to save your game, resume rounds, and apply your settings. Selecting an experience does not require submitting a birth date or your name to us. We do not receive this local game information through the current released-build configuration.

Spoken word prompts use Apple's device speech services. The app does not record your voice or access the microphone, contacts, photos, location, or advertising identifier. There is no advertising, purchase processing, third-party analytics SDK, or cross-app tracking in the current iOS app. We do not sell or share personal information for targeted advertising.

## Backups and device security

You can export a JSON progress backup through Files and import one later. Backups include game progress and rewards, but exclude experience settings and online authentication tokens. The location and service you choose, including iCloud Drive, receive the file at your direction and handle it under their own terms and privacy policies. Anyone who can read your backup can read its contents; keep it private. The app does not provide automatic cross-device progress synchronization.

Family backup and experience controls use Apple's device-owner authentication. The app receives the authentication result, not your passcode or biometric data. This control does not verify parental identity or obtain verified parental consent.

## Children and family play

Family play keeps game progress on the device and does not create a Firebase identity or connect to online teams, friend challenges, or public standings. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through family play. Parents and guardians can manage exported backups and contact us with questions. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us so we can review and remove it where applicable.

## Development-only online play

In Debug simulator builds, players selecting the 13+ experience can connect to developer-operated local Firebase emulators. These features process a guest identifier, preset nickname, submitted spellings, scores, challenge participation, and team participation to operate online games. An authentication session is stored in device-only Keychain and is excluded from exported backups. Challenge participants can see nicknames and final scores; invitation codes should be treated as shared access. These simulator features are not connected to a production online service in the current iOS configuration and should not be used with real children's online data.

## Retention and deletion

Local progress remains until app data is removed or replaced. Removing the app using iOS Delete App removes its local game files; offloading an app can preserve its data. Exported files and device or cloud backups must be deleted separately through the service holding them. Keychain items can survive app deletion. The development online-account deletion control requests removal of the test identity from the local backend and clears its saved session; it leaves Adventure progress on the device. Contact us if you need help with a saved test identity.

If you contact support, we receive your email address and the information you send. We use it to respond and retain correspondence only as needed to resolve the request, meet legal obligations, or address disputes. Avoid sending passwords, sensitive information, or children's identifying details. Our email provider processes correspondence to deliver this service. We may disclose information we hold where legally required or reasonably necessary to protect rights and security. We do not otherwise provide local game progress to outside parties.

## Your choices and rights

You can play offline, manage backups, remove local app data, and contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of information you have sent us. Depending on your location, you may have additional rights or the right to complain to a privacy regulator. We may request information reasonably needed to verify a request. We cannot retrieve game files stored only on your device or delete files from your chosen backup provider.

Apple independently handles App Store transactions, device backups, and any diagnostics you choose to share under Apple's settings and [privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). No method of storage or transmission can guarantee absolute security.

## Changes

We will publish updates here with a revised effective date. Before introducing production online data collection or materially different data practices, we will update this policy and provide any notice or consent required by law.
